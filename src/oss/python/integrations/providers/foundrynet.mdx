[FoundryNet](https://foundrynet.io) builds MINT Protocol, an on-chain work-attestation layer for AI agents: register an identity, attest completed work, and verify trust—settled on Solana and verifiable by anyone.

## Integrations

- [MINT Attest](/oss/integrations/callbacks/mint_attest) - On-chain attestation for every LangChain chain or agent run.
