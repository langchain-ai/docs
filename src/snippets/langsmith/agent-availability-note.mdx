<Note>
Agent-based workspaces are generally available and are rolling out to new organizations in stages. To check whether your workspace is agent-based, look at the control at the top left. In an agent-based workspace, it is a workspace switcher that names your workspace. In a [project-based](/langsmith/observability-concepts#tracing-projects) workspace, it shows the LangSmith logo. An existing project-based workspace does not convert automatically, but LangChain can convert it on request. To ask about access, [contact our sales team](https://www.langchain.com/contact-sales).
</Note>
