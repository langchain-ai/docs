Smithtune is a command-line tool for fine-tuning models on data recorded in LangSmith. It integrates with [Fireworks](https://fireworks.ai/) and [Baseten](https://www.baseten.co/) for training, evaluation, and deployment.

Start with a tracing project or a dataset of [trajectories](/langsmith/observability-concepts#trajectories). A trajectory is one recorded conversation, including its tool calls and results. Images, audio, and video are not supported. You also need access to Fireworks or Baseten for training.

## Follow the workflow

Smithtune takes a conversation dataset through preparation, training, and evaluation:

1. **Select conversations.** Use filters to select examples from a tracing project. Add model judging to review their content against your criteria. Skip this step if you already have a trajectory dataset.
2. **Prepare data.** Check messages and available tools, then split the data for training, validation, and testing. Each conversation stays in one split.
3. **Train a model.** Preview the run, then fine-tune a supported model with Fireworks or Baseten.
4. **Compare results.** Evaluate the base and tuned models on test conversations. Open the LangSmith comparison link to inspect responses and scores.
5. **Deploy an endpoint (optional).** Serve the tuned model for your application. Evaluation does not require a separate `deploy` command.


## Get started

Follow the [Smithtune README](https://github.com/langchain-ai/smithtune#readme) for installation, supported models, and commands. It includes the setup needed for dataset access, training, and model judging.
