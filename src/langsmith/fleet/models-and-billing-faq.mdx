Fleet provides three managed model tiers. The model behind each tier may change over time as new models become available, so you can choose based on the work you need done instead of configuring a specific provider or model.

| Mode | Best for | Relative cost |
| ---- | -------- | ------------- |
| **Fast** | Everyday tasks such as research, summaries, and drafting | Lowest |
| **Pro** | More complex tasks that benefit from stronger reasoning | Higher |
| **Max** | The most demanding tasks, where maximum capability matters most | Highest |

For most tasks, start with **Fast**. Move to **Pro** when a task needs more careful reasoning. Use **Max** for your hardest work, where capability matters more than speed or cost.

## How is Fleet metered?

Fleet usage is measured in LangChain Compute Units (LCUs). LCU usage is based on the model work your agent performs, including the selected mode and the amount of content it processes and generates. The free allowance is shared across your organization and resets monthly:

- **Free plan**: 5 LCUs included per organization each month. After the allowance is used, Fleet pauses new runs until the allowance resets or the organization upgrades to Plus.
- **Plus plan**: 25 LCUs included per organization each month. Additional usage is billed.

## Does every Fleet run cost the same?

No. A Fleet run can make multiple model calls, and tasks vary in length and complexity. A longer task, a larger amount of context, or a higher-capability mode can consume more LCUs than a short task in Fast mode.

## What are Fast, Pro, and Max?

Fast, Pro, and Max are performance tiers, not fixed model names. Fleet selects and maintains the underlying models for each tier, based on specific task performance and cost optimization.

- **Fast** prioritizes speed and cost efficiency for everyday work.
- **Pro** provides stronger reasoning for more complex work.
- **Max** provides the highest capability for the most demanding work and typically costs more and takes longer.

## Why can I no longer bring my own model?

Managed Fleet now includes models out of the box. You no longer need to choose a provider, configure a model, or supply a provider API key.

The managed tiers let LangChain evaluate models with Fleet, provide a more consistent experience, and update the model behind a tier as better options become available. Custom models are therefore not available alongside Fast, Pro, and Max in the managed Fleet model picker.

If custom models are a requirement for an enterprise deployment, contact your LangChain account team or [reach out to sales](https://www.langchain.com/contact-sales).

## Do I need my own model-provider API key?

No. LangChain manages model-provider access for Fast, Pro, and Max. Your organization is charged for Fleet usage in LCUs.

## When does this change take effect?

The new model tiers and LCU pricing apply to new Fleet usage starting **July 15, 2026**. Organizations already using Fleet before that date will transition to the new model on October 1, 2026.

If your organization has grandfathered Plus seat or trace pricing, those rates do not change when Fleet moves to LCU pricing.

For current rates, see the [LangSmith pricing page](https://www.langchain.com/pricing).
