A team is a group of organization members that shares workspace access. Instead of assigning a role to each user in each workspace, you give a team a role once, and every member receives it. Teams build on [role-based access control (RBAC)](/langsmith/rbac) and [attribute-based access control (ABAC)](/langsmith/abac).

<Note>
Teams are available for organizations on the [Enterprise plan](https://www.langchain.com/pricing) and for [self-hosted](/langsmith/self-hosted) deployments. [Contact our sales team](https://www.langchain.com/contact-sales) to learn more.
</Note>

## Team types

LangSmith has two kinds of teams:

- **Organization team**: A team you create in the organization settings. You give it a [workspace role](/langsmith/rbac#workspace-roles) in one or more workspaces, and its members receive that role in each workspace they belong to.
- **Workspace team**: The part of an organization team that belongs to one workspace. Giving an organization team access to a workspace creates its workspace team there. Removing that access deletes it.

A workspace team lets a workspace's administrators manage access in their workspace without organization-level permissions. It starts with no members. Its members receive only the [access policies](/langsmith/abac) attached to it.

For example, the `Support` organization team has the [Workspace Editor](/langsmith/rbac#workspace-editor) role in `Production`. A [Workspace Admin](/langsmith/rbac#workspace-admin) in `Production` can add more users to the `Support` workspace team there and attach a policy that lets the workspace team's members read projects tagged `Environment=Development`.

## Access rules

A user's access in a workspace combines these sources:

| Source | What it contributes |
|--------|--------------------|
| The user's own workspace role | The role's permissions and access policies. |
| Organization teams the user belongs to that have access to the workspace | Each team's role in the workspace, that role's access policies, and the team's access policies. |
| Workspace teams the user belongs to in the workspace | Each workspace team's access policies. |

LangSmith combines the sources as follows:

- **Permissions**: The user holds every permission from their own role and from each team's role.
- **Access policies**: LangSmith evaluates policies from all sources together. A matching deny policy from any source blocks access, even when another source allows the action. For the evaluation order, refer to [How ABAC works with RBAC](/langsmith/abac#how-abac-works-with-rbac).

A team's access applies only to users who are members of the workspace. Giving a team access does not add anyone to the workspace. To give a team member access, [add the user to the workspace](/langsmith/set-up-hierarchy#set-up-a-workspace) or [sync the team from your identity provider](#sync-team-members-from-your-identity-provider). A team member who joins the workspace later receives the team's role.

## Before you begin

- Managing teams in the UI requires [RBAC](/langsmith/rbac) and [ABAC](/langsmith/abac) for the organization. Without ABAC, team access policies cannot be attached and have no effect, but team roles still apply.
- To attach access policies, [create the policies](/langsmith/abac#managing-access-policies) and [tag your resources](/langsmith/set-up-resource-tags) first.
- Creating teams and giving them workspace access requires the [Organization Admin](/langsmith/rbac#organization-admin) or [Organization Operator](/langsmith/rbac#organization-operator) role. For the permission each action checks, refer to [Permissions](#permissions).

## Enable teams for self-hosted deployments

<Note>
On [self-hosted](/langsmith/self-hosted) deployments, teams require Helm chart TBD or later (application version TBD).
</Note>

Use one of the following options to enable teams:

- **Enable for a specific organization**: Run the following against your LangSmith PostgreSQL database, replacing `<organization_id>` with the ID copied from the organization settings page in the UI:

    ```sql
    UPDATE organizations SET config = config || '{"can_use_teams": true}' WHERE id = '<organization_id>' AND NOT is_personal;
    ```

- **Enable for all organizations**: Add the following environment variable to `commonEnv` in your `values.yaml`:

    ```yaml
    DEFAULT_ORG_FEATURE_CAN_USE_TEAMS: "true"
    ```

To manage teams in the UI, also [enable ABAC](/langsmith/abac#enable-abac-for-self-hosted-deployments).

## Create a team

To create a team:

1. Navigate to Organization **Settings** > **Teams**.
1. Click **Create team**.
1. Enter a **Name** and, optionally, a **Description**.
1. (Optional) Add members, workspace access, and access policies. Refer to [Edit a team](#edit-a-team).
1. Click **Create team**.

Team names must be unique in the organization and are case-sensitive.

## Edit a team

To edit a team:

1. Navigate to Organization **Settings** > **Teams** and click the team.
1. Make your changes in **Members**, **Workspaces**, or **Access policies**.
1. Click **Update team**.

LangSmith saves the changes together. If one fails, LangSmith keeps the unsaved changes so you can retry them.

### Members

Click **+ Add member** to add organization members. To remove a member, click the remove icon on their row. A removed member loses the access the team gave them.

The section also lists users who belong only to one of the team's workspace teams, with a link to that workspace team.

### Workspaces

Click **+ Grant workspace** and select a workspace to give the team's members a role there. The role defaults to [Workspace Viewer](/langsmith/rbac#workspace-viewer). You can select any built-in or [custom](/langsmith/rbac#custom-roles) workspace role instead, and change it later on the workspace's row.

To remove access, click the remove icon on the workspace's row and type the workspace name to confirm. Removing access deletes the team's workspace team in that workspace, with its members and access policies.

### Access policies

Click **+ Attach policy** to attach an [access policy](/langsmith/abac#access-policy-structure). An organization team's policies apply in every workspace the team can access. A workspace team's policies apply only in its workspace.

To limit an organization team's policy to one workspace, attach it through the [API](#manage-teams-with-the-api) with `workspace_id` set. The team must have access to that workspace, and you must be a member of that workspace. The team dialog shows the workspace that such a policy applies in.

## Manage a workspace team

Users with the [Workspace Admin](/langsmith/rbac#workspace-admin) role, or a [custom role](/langsmith/rbac#custom-roles) with the workspace teams [permissions](#permissions), manage the workspace teams in their workspace.

To manage a workspace team:

1. Navigate to the workspace's **Settings** > **Teams**.
1. Click a team.
1. Add or remove members, or attach or detach access policies.
1. Click **Update team**.

The page lists one workspace team for each organization team with access to the workspace. Each shows its organization team's name, description, members, and access policies as read-only, with a link to the organization team. You can add only users who are members of the workspace.

## Delete a team

To delete a team, navigate to Organization **Settings** > **Teams**, click the delete icon on the team's row, and type the team name to confirm.

Deleting a team deletes its workspace teams, members, workspace access, and access policy attachments.

A role that a team uses in a workspace cannot be deleted. Change the team's role or remove its workspace access first.

## Sync team members from your identity provider

Link an organization team to a group in your identity provider (IdP), and LangSmith sets the team's members from that group. Sync works with [SCIM](/langsmith/user-management#set-up-scim-for-your-organization) and [SSO Groups Sync](/langsmith/user-management#sso-groups-sync-alternative). Workspace teams cannot be synced.

A group linked to a team does not need to follow the [`LS:` group naming convention](/langsmith/user-management#group-naming-convention). Each IdP group can be linked to only one team in the organization.

To sync a team:

1. Navigate to Organization **Settings** > **Teams** and click the team.
1. In **General**, enter the group's name in **IdP group**. For SCIM, enter the SCIM group's name. For SSO Groups Sync, enter a value from the [groups claim](/langsmith/user-management#sso-groups-sync-alternative).
1. Select **Sync members from the IdP group**.
1. Click **Update team**.

### Sync behavior

- **Matching**: A group matches when its name equals the team's IdP group, ignoring leading and trailing spaces. Matching is case-sensitive.
- **Timing**: SSO Groups Sync updates a user's teams at each login and requires **Sync workspace/role assignments**. SCIM updates a user's teams when their SCIM groups change.
- **Membership**: A synced team's members are the users in the IdP group. A change you make in LangSmith lasts until that user's next sync.
- **Organization role**: When no `LS:` group sets a user's organization role, a team match sets it:
    - Through SSO Groups Sync, a new user joins the organization as an [Organization Viewer](/langsmith/rbac#organization-viewer). A user whose membership SSO Groups Sync manages is set to Organization Viewer. Other memberships, such as from an invite, keep their role.
    - Through SCIM, a user whose membership SCIM does not manage is set to [Organization User](/langsmith/rbac#organization-user), and SCIM manages the membership after that. This includes users with a higher role, such as Organization Admin.
- **Workspace membership**: Sync adds the user to each workspace the team can access. This membership has no role of its own. The user's access comes from the team's role. At the user's next sync, LangSmith removes the membership if no team has access to that workspace.
- **Login gate**: When **Require matching group to sign in** is enabled, a group that matches a synced team counts as a matching group.
- **Sync disabled**: The team keeps its members, and you manage them in LangSmith.

## Permissions

Each team action checks one permission. Organization teams use organization permissions. Workspace teams use workspace permissions in the team's workspace. `organization:manage` and `workspaces:manage` do not allow these actions.

| Action | Organization team | Workspace team |
|--------|-------------------|----------------|
| View teams, members, and access policies | `organization:teams:read` | `workspaces:teams:read` |
| Create, rename, or delete a team | `organization:teams:manage` | Not available |
| Add or remove members | `organization:teams:manage-members` | `workspaces:teams:manage-members` |
| Attach or detach access policies | `organization:teams:manage-policies` | `workspaces:teams:manage-policies` |
| Give, change, or remove workspace access | `organization:teams:manage-grants` | Not available |

To let users manage workspace teams without the Workspace Admin role, add `workspaces:teams:read`, `workspaces:teams:manage-members`, and `workspaces:teams:manage-policies` to a [custom role](/langsmith/rbac#custom-roles). Attaching a policy in the UI also requires `organization:read`, which lists the organization's access policies.

For the built-in roles that hold each permission, refer to [Teams](/langsmith/organization-workspace-operations#teams) and [Workspace teams](/langsmith/organization-workspace-operations#workspace-teams) in the operations reference.

## Manage teams with the API

The teams endpoints are under `/api/v1/platform/orgs/current/teams`. [Audit logs](/langsmith/audit-logs#tracked-operations-reference) record each change to a team.
