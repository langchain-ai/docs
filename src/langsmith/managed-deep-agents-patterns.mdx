Patterns show how to combine Managed Deep Agents capabilities for common workflows. Use these guides to adapt an agent's instructions, tools, and channels to your use case.

<ManagedDeepAgentsPublicBetaNote />

## Patterns

- [Scheduled agent](/langsmith/managed-deep-agents-tutorial): Extend the quickstart research assistant with a custom search tool, durable memory, and a weekday schedule.
- [Slack watch mode](/langsmith/managed-deep-agents-watch-mode): Build an agent that decides when to reply, schedule a check-in, or stay silent.
- [Goal-oriented coordination agent](/langsmith/managed-deep-agents-goal-oriented): Work toward an ongoing team goal with memory, feedback, and scheduled reviews.
- [Dreaming](/langsmith/managed-deep-agents-dreaming): Review a person's traces on a schedule and update their memory.
- [Agent swarm](/langsmith/managed-deep-agents-swarm): Coordinate async subagents to investigate an incident in parallel.
- [Semantic search](/langsmith/managed-deep-agents-semantic-search): Search document passages by meaning and cite their sources.
