## Patterns

- [Daily digest](/langsmith/managed-deep-agents-tutorial): Remember topics in durable memory and send a weekday news digest.
- [Watch mode](/langsmith/managed-deep-agents-watch-mode): Monitor a Slack channel, stay quiet by default, and schedule follow-up check-ins.
- [Goals](/langsmith/managed-deep-agents-goal-oriented): Schedule recurring progress checks until multi-turn work is done.
- [Dreaming](/langsmith/managed-deep-agents-dreaming): Review a person's traces nightly to update their memory and propose improvements.
- [Swarm](/langsmith/managed-deep-agents-swarm): Fan out an incident investigation across parallel async subagents.
