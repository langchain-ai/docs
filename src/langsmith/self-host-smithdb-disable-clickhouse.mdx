---

After you switch queries to SmithDB, LangSmith keeps writing every new trace to ClickHouse as well, so ClickHouse holds a second copy of your data until you disable it. Disabling ClickHouse stops those writes, stops all reads, and removes the ClickHouse workloads from your installation. For a new installation that never runs ClickHouse, see [Install without ClickHouse](/langsmith/self-host-smithdb-install#install-without-clickhouse) instead.

<Warning>
Any ClickHouse history you did not [migrate to SmithDB](/langsmith/self-host-smithdb-migrate) becomes unavailable once ClickHouse is disabled.
</Warning>

## Before you begin

- **Version**: Run LangSmith 0.17 or later.
- **SDK migration**: Complete [Migrate to SmithDB-backed SDK methods](/langsmith/smithdb-sdk-migration) first. The deprecated methods stop working once ClickHouse is off and return HTTP `501`.
- **SmithDB performance**: Run with queries on SmithDB through a full 24-hour cycle and confirm ingestion and query performance hold at peak. Tune with [Configure SmithDB for scale](/langsmith/self-host-smithdb-scale) before continuing.
- **Observability**: Configure [SmithDB observability](/langsmith/self-host-smithdb-observability) so you can confirm ingestion and query health after the change.

## Disable ClickHouse

Contact LangChain through the [Support Portal](https://support.langchain.com/) to plan the change. The team reviews your cutover status, provides the Helm changes for your ClickHouse setup, whether chart-managed, external, or LangSmith-managed, and advises on backing up and retaining the ClickHouse data afterward.

Once ClickHouse is disabled, LangSmith no longer reads from or writes to it. Requests that still need ClickHouse return HTTP `501` naming the feature, and full-text search over blob storage (`config.blobStorage.chSearchEnabled`) turns off.
