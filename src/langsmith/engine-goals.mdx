<Note>
Goals are available only on LangSmith Cloud. They are not available on self-hosted LangSmith or BYOC.
</Note>

A goal tells [LangSmith Engine](/langsmith/engine) what you want your agent to get better at, such as answering more questions correctly or costing less per conversation. Each goal has a metric that measures it from your traces. Engine focuses its recommendations on the goal, and the metric's chart shows whether your changes improve it. You set up a goal in a conversation with [LangSmith Chat](/langsmith/chat), which reviews your recent traces and helps you define the goal, its metric, and how to track it.

## How Engine uses goals

Goals give Engine a measurable definition of success. For each goal, it compares the traces that score best and worst on the goal's metric and investigates what separates them. For example, for a goal to increase the percentage of emails sent without edits, Engine can investigate how the agent's behavior differs between emails that were accepted and emails that needed changes. Engine files relevant findings under the goal as [recommendations](/langsmith/engine-issues). These can address bugs, inefficient workflows, or ways the agent falls short of the user's intended outcome.

The **Engine** page groups recommendations by goal. Recommendations that are not filed under a goal appear under **General**. Engine still makes recommendations outside your goals, so a goal focuses Engine without hiding other problems.

## Create a goal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the goal chat">
    Select your agent and go to **Engine**. Select the environment from the environment switcher at the top of the page. Under **Goals** on the overview, click **Create a goal**.

    While Engine runs its first analysis after [setup](/langsmith/engine-setup), you can also click **Create goal** under **Create a new goal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to improve">
    Chat analyzes your agent's recent traces and suggests goals. Choose one of the suggestions, or describe what you want your agent to improve in your own words. Chat may ask a clarifying question when the answer changes the goal or how it is measured.

    Once you choose, Chat proposes the goal's name and description, and LangSmith saves the goal as a draft. Drafts are visible only to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to measure it">
    Chat recommends metrics for the goal, or you can describe how you want to measure it. The metric appears under **How we measure this** in the goal preview beside the chat. For the kinds of metrics available, see [Choose a metric](#choose-a-metric).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up tracking">
    If your traces do not already record the metric, choose how to record it under **Tracking**. See [Set up tracking](#set-up-tracking). You can also choose **Skip for now** and add tracking later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the goal">
    Click **Create goal**. The goal becomes active and appears on the overview for everyone with access to the agent environment. Engine then looks through its open recommendations from the last week that are not filed under a goal, and files the ones that fit the new goal under it. The goal shows **Finding relevant recommendations** while this runs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change the goal, metric, or tracking before you create the goal, click **Edit** next to that step in the goal preview, or tell Chat what to change. If Chat finds that an open goal already covers your request, it offers to use or edit that goal, or to create a separate one.

The goal chat counts as Engine usage. If your organization or agent environment has reached its [spend limit](/langsmith/engine#set-spend-limits-and-monitor-usage), you cannot use the goal chat until the limit is raised or the next billing period begins.

## Choose a metric

A goal's metric is one number, computed from your traces, that shows whether the goal is improving. Each metric has a direction, either increase or decrease.

A metric can read:

- **Built-in values**: Cost, latency, time to first token, or tokens, which every run records. Conversations also record the number of turns.
- **Feedback**: A score that an [online evaluator](/langsmith/online-evaluations-llm-as-judge), your application, or a reviewer writes. A pass or fail score reports a rate, and a numeric score reports an average or a percentile.
- **Conditions**: The rate or count of units that match a filter, such as tool calls that end in an error, or conversations that include a successful booking.
- **Counts**: How many runs occur, in total or per trace or conversation, such as model calls per trace.

For example:

- **Fewer escalations**: The rate of traces with a pass or fail `escalated` feedback score that your application writes when a request escalates.
- **Lower cost per conversation**: The average cost of a conversation.
- **Fewer conversations with a failed tool call**: The rate of conversations that contain a tool run that ended in an error.

Some outcomes arrive after a trace ends, such as whether a pull request your agent opened was merged. Chat can mark the last few days (up to 7) of such a metric as still settling, so the chart does not report them as final.

## Set up tracking

Where your metric's data comes from decides whether it needs tracking:

- **Already on your traces**: Built-in values, run names, inputs, outputs, statuses, metadata, and feedback that something already writes need no setup. The **Tracking** step shows **Already on your traces** or **Recorded on every run**.
- **Computed by an evaluator**: When an evaluator can score the metric from each run or conversation, Chat drafts an online evaluator and, for most metrics, tests it on recent runs. Review the draft, then click **Create evaluator**. The evaluator scores matching new traces in the background.
- **Recorded by your code**: When the outcome is not on your traces, such as a closed deal, code has to record it. Choose one of:
  - **Have Engine generate a PR**: Engine files a tracking recommendation under the goal and opens a pull request that records the signal in your agent's code. This requires a [connected repository](/langsmith/engine-repositories).
  - **I’ll add it myself**: Get instructions and code to add to your agent.
  - **Skip for now**: Create the goal without tracking and set it up later.

You do not need to wait for metric data before you create a goal. LangSmith keeps checking for data in the background. To change a tracking choice later, click **Edit** next to **Tracking** on the goal's page, or **Add tracking** on a goal whose tracking you skipped.

## Track progress

Each active goal on the overview charts its metric for the last 14 days and compares the latest settled week with the week before. Click a goal to open its page, which shows the chart and the recommendations filed under the goal.

When a goal has no chart, a badge shows what it is waiting on:

- **Not tracked**: No tracking is set up, or the evaluator or pull request that records the metric is not in place yet. Click **Add tracking** or **Review tracking** to finish setup.
- **Waiting for data**: Tracking is in place, and the metric has not arrived on new traces yet.
- **Couldn’t check**: LangSmith could not read the goal's data. Click **Try again** on the goal.

If a goal's metric stops arriving, for example because an evaluator was deleted or turned off, the goal offers the same setup steps again.

## Manage goals

Open the actions menu on a goal's card, or open the goal's page, to:

- **Edit**: Reopen the goal chat to change the metric or tracking. On an active goal, Chat's proposals wait until you click **Save changes**. A goal's name and description cannot change after you create it. To pursue a different outcome, archive the goal and create a new one.
- **Mark as completed**: Move the goal and its recommendations off the active board. To review a completed goal and its recommendation history, click **View completed goals** on the overview.
- **Archive**: Close a goal you no longer want to pursue.
- **Delete**: Permanently delete the goal. Its recommendations move to **General**. This cannot be undone.

Two active goals on the same agent environment cannot use the same metric. When you ask for a goal that an open goal already covers, Chat points you to it.

## Required permissions

Creating a goal requires a [Workspace Admin or Workspace Editor](/langsmith/rbac#workspace-roles) role, or a custom role that includes `issues:create` and `issues:update`. Editing a goal through Chat also requires both, and deleting a goal requires `issues:delete`.
